1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:
This project transitions enterprise vulnerability management from traditional, static 
CVSS scoring to a Risk-Based Vulnerability Management (RBVM) approach. By synthesizing 
scanner telemetry with dynamic threat intelligence (EPSS, CISA KEV) and asset criticality, 
the RBVM engine eliminates alert fatigue and isolates high-risk threats requiring 
immediate 24-hour remediation.

2. DATASET METHODOLOGY:
Due to enterprise data privacy policies regarding internal network topology, 
a synthetic dataset (N=5,000) was programmatically generated using Python (numpy/pandas). 
The dataset accurately models realistic security distributions, including right-skewed 
EPSS probabilities (Beta distribution) and CISA Known Exploited Vulnerability (KEV) ratios.

3. KEY EDA FINDINGS:
- Less than 10% of total vulnerabilities exhibit active exploit potential (EPSS > 0.35).
- CVSS scores alone create severe noise: numerous CVSS 9.0+ vulnerabilities lack exploit 
  activity, while targeted lower-CVSS bugs (6.0–7.5) are actively exploited in the wild.
- Internet-facing assets account for >70% of total high-risk exposure.

4. BASELINE MODEL RESULTS:
- Model Selected: Decision Tree Classifier (max_depth=5)
- Primary Metric: Recall (Target Class: Action Required) = ~0.94
- ROC-AUC Score: ~0.96
- Business Impact: Reduces standard patching volume by >75% while capturing ~94% 
  of critical, actively exploited threats.
